# Google consolidation

Use the primary business Google identity supplied separately. Keep account addresses, credentials and recovery information in restricted storage. Retain the current YouTube login until channel ownership and required operations are checked.

1. Recover access to the discontinued Workspace tenant through its administrator or former provider where possible. Identify remaining Drive, email, Calendar, shared-drive and offline copies. Back up recoverable data and verify it opens.
2. Inventory owners, managers and recovery contacts for Drive, Calendar, Business Profile, Analytics and Search Console. Review existing verification and ownership dependencies.
3. Propose least-privilege access under the primary business identity. Obtain explicit approval before modifying permissions or transferring ownership. Confirm access with both existing and new administrators before any retirement.
4. Verify the channel and possible YouTube Brand Account. Check that the primary business identity can perform required operations through approved permissions before considering account retirement. Verify any videos proposed for publication.
5. To move booking from Calendly, create and test a Google Calendar appointment schedule with correct availability, time zone, confirmation, Meet and privacy settings. Test with an external invitee, then set `SITE_BOOKING_URL` to the verified scheduling URL and redeploy after approval.

Account deletion, permission changes and ownership transfers require explicit authorisation. No account access or recovery was verified for this implementation.
