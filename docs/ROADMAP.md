## Google identity consolidation and recovery

Use the designated primary business Google identity from the private project brief. Keep a private inventory of owners, administrators, backup contacts and multi-factor recovery methods. Confirm that the primary login is recoverable before depending on it. The channel login remains active until access and permissions have been independently checked.

1. Determine whether the discontinued Workspace organisation and its users still exist. Check the original admin account, subscription status, previous provider or reseller, recovery contacts and available support or restoration options. Avoid promising recovery before verifying the state of the tenant.
2. Inventory surviving copies in user Drive, shared drives, old computers, synced folders, existing Takeout archives, mailbox clients, backups and authorised collaborators' copies. Export recoverable material to approved storage, record its source and check that sampled documents and messages can be opened.
3. Review Drive sharing, Calendar ownership and appointment dependencies, Business Profile manager and owner roles, GA4 account and property access, and Search Console users and verification methods. Propose the appropriate least-privilege roles for the primary identity. Use targeted sharing or delegated access where supported and obtain explicit approval for each ownership transfer.
4. Check YouTube channel identity, permissions and whether it is associated with a Brand Account. Verify that the primary identity can perform the required channel operations after an approved permission change. Preserve the existing login and recovery options until the owner signs off.
5. Validate access from authorized sessions and record owner, administrator, backup and recovery routes privately. Archive original records according to the business retention requirements. Account deletion and ownership changes require explicit approval.
