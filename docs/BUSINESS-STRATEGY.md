# Business strategy

Gavin Ferns Business Consulting and Coaching trades publicly as Gavin Ferns Consulting. The business offers commercial advice and coaching with digital transformation, AI and automation expertise backed by senior programme delivery experience.

Established SMEs typically turning over £250,000–£1 million are the primary audience, alongside funded founders and owners dealing with weak marketing, profitability, operational efficiency or reliance on their day-to-day involvement. Bespoke corporate work serves rail, infrastructure, construction and engineering teams.

The discovery call establishes the challenge, desired outcome, commercial fit and decision timetable. Assess acquisition, retention and order value alongside gross margins, operating costs, implementation capacity and risk. Judge experiments using real data and review dates. Avoid promises of increased revenue or profit.

The qualification band begins at £2,500. Published consulting fees begin at £2,997 and each paid engagement must exceed £2,500. Confirm the scope, VAT treatment and third-party costs before offering terms.
