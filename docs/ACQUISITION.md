# Client acquisition and sales operations

The profitability campaign is available at `/campaign/profitability`. Use [campaign configuration](../lib/campaigns.ts) to maintain the headline, advertising message, call to action, title, description and optional local image with alt text. The Three Multipliers, business owner dependency and AI and business automation templates remain inactive and return 404. Use approved creative assets in `public/` and confirm copy and publication rights before activating another campaign.

## Lead capture and access

1. Create a business-owned PostgreSQL database. Use a separate database for preview and testing. Keep TLS verification enabled on hosted connections, enable backups and test restoration. The application role needs read and write access to the acquisition tables. Use a separate migration account with schema ownership.
2. Set `ACQUISITION_ADMIN_DATABASE_URL` privately and run `node scripts/acquisition-admin.mjs migrate`. Apply changes to preview first. Set `ACQUISITION_DATABASE_URL` to the runtime connection and set `ACQUISITION_SECURITY_KEY` to a securely generated secret of at least 32 characters.
3. Provision individual accounts with `node scripts/acquisition-admin.mjs user sales <email>` or `node scripts/acquisition-admin.mjs user advertising <email>`. Pipe a unique password from a password manager or a hidden shell prompt. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes. Provisioning an existing user replaces their role and password and revokes their sessions. Revoke an account with `node scripts/acquisition-admin.mjs revoke <email>`.
4. Set `SITE_ACQUISITION_ORIGIN` to the exact HTTPS origin, with no trailing slash. Enable `SITE_ACQUISITION_ENABLED` only after reviewing copy, storage, access and retention. When capture is disabled, the local discovery brief remains available. It reveals the calendar after completion for budgets at or above £3,000. People with an unagreed budget can share their brief for manual review. When capture is enabled, primary enquiries go to the three-step application and only qualified leads receive a private calendar handoff.
5. Use `/sales` to review applications and select a stage. The advertising role sees campaign and channel totals by date. Contract values, payment amounts, delivery costs and contribution are available only to sales. It cannot read lead details, change records or download the pipeline CSV. The business retains control of its hosting, database, source code, domains, Trello, Zoho and advertising accounts. Use individual delegated accounts and revoke access when the work ends.

Sessions expire after eight hours and are revoked at sign-out or account revocation. Application and sign-in submissions are rate limited. On Vercel the app uses the platform-forwarded IP header. For other hosts, set `SITE_TRUSTED_CLIENT_IP_HEADER` only to a header your ingress overwrites. Rate-limit identifiers are hashed. Confirm the login and both permission levels on a protected preview. Keep production database credentials, provisioning access and scheduler secrets with the owner.

Application submission stores the required privacy acknowledgement and version. Marketing consent is separate and optional. The marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. The lead detail screen supports deletion of the lead and its pending email records. Date-and-campaign totals and commercial records remain for business reporting. Confirm the retention policy before production. Run `node scripts/acquisition-admin.mjs prune <approved-retention-days>` as a business-owned maintenance job to remove expired sessions and eligible enquiries at the approved age. Confirm the impact on attribution reports and backups when applying retention.

## Booking and sales milestones

Set `SITE_BOOKING_URL` to the verified Calendly or Google Calendar appointment scheduling URL. In the sales dashboard, mark the lead as qualified and copy the private booking link, or enable the qualified Zoho email. Booking links expire 30 days after qualification. The link records a handoff when the applicant continues to the calendar.

For Calendly, configure an invitee-created webhook for `POST /api/acquisition/calendly` with `CALENDLY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. The signed payload must carry the opaque booking reference in `tracking.utm_content` and the applicant must book with the same email address. Retry deliveries are safe. For Google Calendar, or if a Calendly booking cannot be matched, confirm the booking with the provider and set the lead to Appointments. Review cancellations and reschedules in the calendar and update the lead stage as needed. Booking history remains recorded.

Use the lead record to mark appointment attendance and move the lead through Proposals, Won, Lost or Nurture. Moving to Proposals records a proposal and moving to Won records a signed contract. Use these stages only after the corresponding milestone has occurred. Enter contracted revenue, payment receipts and delivery costs separately. Record actual ad expenditure by campaign from the advertising account or invoice. Removing a financial entry voids it in reports and retains the audit record. The site does not create ads, change budgets or initiate payments.

The sales-only Trello export contains card name, target list, opaque lead reference, campaign and date. Map the stage codes to your owned board's lists with your CSV importer or a controlled import script. The dashboard export excludes email, names, application answers and financial figures. Sales can explicitly request contact columns with `?contact=yes` if the board’s access and handling have been approved.

## Email automation with Zoho

Configure a business-owned Zoho Mail OAuth client, choose the correct data centre, and provision a verified sender and a token scoped to send Zoho Mail messages. Set `ZOHO_REGION`, `ZOHO_CLIENT_ID`, `ZOHO_CLIENT_SECRET`, `ZOHO_REFRESH_TOKEN`, `ZOHO_MAIL_ACCOUNT_ID` and `ZOHO_FROM_EMAIL`. Supported region keys are `eu`, `us`, `uk`, `in`, `au`, `ca` and `jp`. Check the account endpoints against the selected Zoho data centre before enabling.

Enable `SITE_ZOHO_AUTOMATION_ENABLED` and select entries in `SITE_ZOHO_TEMPLATES` with a comma-separated list of `acknowledgement`, `qualified` and `nurture`. Set `SITE_ZOHO_NURTURE_DELAY_DAYS` if needed. The default delay is one day. The nurture message checks current marketing consent and stage at send time. Review and edit template wording in [the integration module](../lib/sales-integrations.ts).

Configure a business-owned scheduler to POST to `/api/acquisition/mail` with `Authorization: Bearer <SALES_JOB_SECRET>`. Generate a distinct secret of at least 32 characters. The queue prevents duplicate jobs for a lead and template. It claims up to four jobs per call. Authentication failures leave jobs pending. A failed or interrupted send requires reconciliation against Zoho before any manual resend. Status is visible in the lead record. Verify delivery, unsubscribe and the qualified link with a controlled mailbox.

## Measurement and campaign profitability

- Set `SITE_ATTRIBUTION_ENABLED` to enable first-party measurement after visitor permission. UTM parameters and referring domains persist for up to 90 days in the browser after opt-in. First touch is the earliest recorded source. Last touch is the latest tagged, campaign landing or external referral source. A subsequent direct visit preserves the last known source. The lead record stores the available pair with the submission, and an aggregate channel view is available to both roles.
- Set `SITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` for direct GA4 and `SITE_GTM_ID` for a business-owned GTM container. The data layer supplies `gf_website_visit`, `gf_landing_visit`, `gf_calculator_interaction` and `gf_application_completed`. Application starts are measured by the consented first-party endpoint. Direct GA4 uses `page_view`, `landing_visit`, `calculator_interaction` and `generate_lead`. Use either direct GA4 or equivalent GTM tags for each measurement to prevent duplicates. Disable automatic history, form and enhanced measurement when they would duplicate the intended events. Never configure GTM to read application fields, calculator figures, private pages or sales records.
- Set `SITE_META_PIXEL_ID` for the Meta browser Lead event and consented campaign PageView. Configure `META_GRAPH_VERSION` and `META_CONVERSIONS_ACCESS_TOKEN` for the server event. They share an opaque event ID for deduplication. The server event is sent only with advertising consent and an available Meta browser or click identifier. It supplies permitted browser metadata and the event ID. No lead contact details, turnover, margins, financial figures or application answers are sent.
- Set `SITE_GOOGLE_ADS_ID` and `SITE_GOOGLE_ADS_LEAD_LABEL` for the receipt-page conversion. Configure the action for submitted applications and leave enhanced conversions and customer data collection off. A completed application is the advertising conversion. Qualification, contracts, payments, delivery costs and contract values stay in the first-party sales system.
- Use campaign slugs in `utm_campaign` and approved, non-personal codes for the other UTM fields. Use separate measurement and advertising consent checks and confirm that withdrawal stops optional tracking and removes related browser state. Stored business records and aggregated historical measures follow the retention policy.
- The dashboard shows events and actual amounts by date in UK time. Website and calculator metrics cover opted-in activity. Applications and milestones count actual actions recorded in the sales system. CAC is advertising expenditure divided by signed contracts. Contribution is contract revenue minus delivery costs and advertising expenditure. Contribution margin divides that by contract revenue. Payments are shown separately. These are period totals rather than a lead-cohort profitability measure. Use the same date range and campaign grouping when reconciling with platform exports.

## Before launch

Run lint, type checking, unit tests, the build and the Chromium suites in the README. Run the acquisition browser test against a disposable PostgreSQL database with generated local credentials and an intercepted calendar. Confirm the live booking provider, email sender, permissions, tracking and consent against the actual business accounts in a protected preview. Review rate limiting, backup and retention configuration. Obtain approval for production deployment and separate explicit approval before launching or funding advertising.
