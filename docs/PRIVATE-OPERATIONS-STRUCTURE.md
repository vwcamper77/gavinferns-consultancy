# Private consultancy workspace structure

This is a blank setup guide. Create the actual workspace in access-controlled business storage. Keep client records, Trello boards, meeting links, templates and contracts out of the site repository. Set permissions per engagement.

| Record | Minimum contents |
| --- | --- |
| Onboarding | Agreed scope, exclusions, owners, communications, confidentiality, data access and consent |
| Discovery report | Objectives, business context, constraints, decisions and unresolved questions |
| Growth audit and baseline | Source, period, customers, frequency, order value, gross margin, operating costs and data quality |
| Strategy and 90-day plan | Prioritised interventions, owners, dependencies, costs, dates and success measures |
| Trello delivery board | Private access, work owner, due date, status, blockers and approved evidence links |
| Monthly review and client report | Baseline comparison, learning, risks, spend, decisions, actions and next review |
| Scheduling and Google Meet | Approved booking link, invitations, permissions, meeting notes and recording agreement if relevant |
| Consulting proposal | Scope and deliverables, excluded implementation spend, fees and VAT, milestones and acceptance |
| Contract checklist | Approved terms, confidentiality, data handling, intellectual property, liability and exit procedures |

Review this structure against the November 2024 operating procedure when supplied. Flag the reported five-year non-compete and non-solicitation provisions for qualified legal review before reuse. The original provisions were not inspected. Keep commercial performance promises out of proposals. Record approval before using client data in AI tools, and document the purpose, retention, access and review controls.
