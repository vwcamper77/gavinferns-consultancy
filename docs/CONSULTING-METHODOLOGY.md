# Consulting methodology

Revenue = Customers × Purchase Frequency × Average Order Value.

- Customers. Measure unique purchasers over a consistent period, then review segments, channels, acquisition cost and conversion.
- Purchase frequency. Divide transactions by customers for the same period and investigate repeat buying, retention, reactivation and customer experience.
- Average order value. Divide total revenue by transactions for the same period and evaluate pricing, bundles, upsell, sales process and margins.

Keep the period, currency, revenue definition, treatment of refunds and VAT consistent. Compare the effect of changes against gross margin, costs, cash needs and ability to deliver. Use a sensitivity range for uncertain inputs. The calculator assumes the multipliers can be varied independently, so use real customer and transaction data to check for interactions, seasonality and delivery constraints.

## Signature programme

1. Discovery and Growth Audit. Agree objectives, data sources, baseline measures and priority constraints.
2. Strategic Implementation and Execution. Prioritise interventions, assign owners and measure progress. Assess AI and automation where there is an evidenced process need and suitable data handling, then pilot with oversight and agreed success measures.
3. Review, Optimisation and Scaling. Compare outcomes with baseline, decide what to continue and set a 6–12 month roadmap.
